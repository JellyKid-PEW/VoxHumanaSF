## High-value corrections from this audit

### SHIP TEXT EVIDENCE — Nova's signal-listening nest is explicitly located

**Book 3 Chapter 6 — `VH1_B3_06v2`**

The secondary listening array is explicitly installed at:

> "port-side equipment crawl, lower aft spine."

The space is described as:
- an old equipment crawl / narrow service pocket;
- "two steps in. One step sideways";
- reached through a hatch;
- dim, warm accidental light;
- one clipped lamp plus two taped light strips under a shelf;
- folded insulation pad used as seating;
- mismatched receiver bank;
- an old cargo panel converted into a desk;
- broken crate used as a side table;
- cable loops on improvised hooks;
- three slates with Nova's frequency labels;
- support bracket / cable management added by Iri;
- connected to an auxiliary storage relay, old atmosphere-sampling stabilizer, thermal regulator, and non-broadcast filter.

The prose explicitly says:

> "Nova had made the crawlspace into a room."

This is the previously remembered **signal-searching / wave-surfing nest** in practical terms, even if the final in-world term "wave surfing" is not used in the chapter.

The same chapter later calls the area the **lower aft spine** and confirms the array is still operating there.

This is aboard the Huntress, not on Garden Station: the corridor outside the hatch is described as humming through "the Huntress's bones," and B.O.B.'s power map identifies it inside Huntress internal load.
